Plagiarism is a major issue in the academic and professional world. It refers to the act of using someone else’s work, ideas or words without giving proper credit to the original author. This can lead to serious consequences, including academic or legal penalties, loss of reputation and even job termination. Therefore, it is important to have tools that can detect plagiarism and help prevent it. One such tool is JetCheck, a plagiarism detection software that uses artificial intelligence (AI) to analyze written content and identify instances of plagiarism. In this article, we will explore the features and benefits of JetCheck, as well as the role of AI in plagiarism detection.
